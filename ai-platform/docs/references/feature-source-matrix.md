## Scope consequence

The approved implementation slice covers A–F. F-003 onboarding, F-007 MCP,
F-008 approvals, full F-009 rollback, F-011 budget enforcement, and F-013
automated drift remediation are deferred. Current implementation and
verification status are in [MVP scope](../architecture/mvp-scope.md) and
[test traceability](../features/test-traceability.md).
